# The paper in ten lines (as the final version reads, `main_iclr2027_final.pdf`)

1. Hyperbolic representation learning rests on a premise, latent hyperbolicity: standard models are already tree-like because their class geometry scores a low Gromov δ.
2. That raw reading is confounded by dimension, by covariance spectrum and by the supremum statistic; a structureless Gaussian cloud reads as tree-like once the dimension is high (Proposition 1).
3. The instrument reads every score as an excess over a Haar null with the same dimension and spectrum, ranks it against 200 replicates, calls a cell genuine under Benjamini–Hochberg, and adds a depth test against a matched star whose false-alarm rate and power are measured on real clouds.
4. On image features, where the premise is read, the excess is not genuine in most cells and removes at most two fifths of the null where it is: the raw reading is not evidence, and the calibrated reading is weak and model-dependent.
5. On class centroids clustered structure is genuine in 49 of 72 cells, 18 of 24 on the two sets with a real hierarchy, in every family; a star of clusters already passes this census, so clustered does not mean deep.
6. The depth test certifies 4 of 12 ImageNet backbones, the same four under a star with the real hubs' spectrum, two of them under every frame; it fires in 0 of 60 runs with randomized hubs and misses a clean implanted tree at ImageNet's noise level, so the eight others are not detected rather than flat. With the real hubs kept and the offsets rotated none of the four fires, so what is certified is hub–offset structure, the coupling between hub arrangement and offsets, not the hub arrangement alone.
7. Leaf-label supervision can produce the depth (augreg ViT-B) or not (DeiT-B), and a backbone trained in hyperbolic space (MERU) reads the same clustering as its Euclidean twin with no detected depth in the near-flat regime.
8. The naive tree map isolates the DINOv2 family as an island, a chaining artifact of Euclidean average linkage; once the cut is controlled a moderate gap remains, every recipe recovers the human taxonomy partially, supervision deepens the resemblance, and the recovery is not WordNet circularity.
9. The self-supervised tree is angular, text depends on recipe, scale and probe (7 of 15 text models genuine on the class names), and what models share is local: neighborhoods, not metrics.
10. Read correctly, foundation models organize classes into clustered structure that is occasionally hierarchical and moderately shared; they do not converge to one common tree, and their raw tree-likeness is not evidence for hyperbolic geometry. The raw reading assigns curvature 0.48 to 2.5 to structureless clouds; raw and calibrated readings predict the zero-cost gain equally well, the depth verdict predicts none, and the gain is +0.9 to +1.3 pp for the contrastive VLMs only: the calibration certifies that there is structure, it does not choose the readout.
