What the cosine study genuinely changes, and the revision will state it this plainly, is the *metric-selection* step: the training objective determines where the tree lives: angular for SSL (sibling triplet agreement 0.91 vs 0.71, cosine vs Euclidean), partly radial for contrastive VLMs (H beats cosine by +0.9..+1.3pp even after L2 normalization). The zero-cost decision tool becomes two-step, both steps free: (i) $\hat\delta$/ORC decide whether to leave Euclidean at all (flat datasets: no); (ii) the objective type selects the metric (SSL → cosine; contrastive VLM → Poincaré; supervised → either).
